## INSTITUTE FOR ACCELERATED MEDICINE
- We are a Wyoming 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We work so every patient can join a pragmatic clinical trial for a promising treatment, with a clinician, at a licensed center, and so every result is published.
### WHO WE ARE
- Accelerated Medicine Foundation Inc, dba Institute for Accelerated Medicine is the legal home of the Right to Trial Initiative. The Institute for Accelerated Medicine is our registered DBA. We also operate as the International Campaign to End War and Disease.
- Right to Trial means patients can reach treatments, providers can deliver them in ordinary care, and the public can see which treatments work. Montana licensed experimental treatment centers. We help other states copy that path and publish comparable results.
