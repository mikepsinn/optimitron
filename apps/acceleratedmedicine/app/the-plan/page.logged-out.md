### WHAT 3.5% MEANS
- A USEFUL BENCHMARK IS NOT A MAGIC SPELL
- POLITICAL SCIENTIST ERICA CHENOWETH FOUND THAT THE NONVIOLENT NATIONAL CAMPAIGNS IN THE STUDY WHICH REACHED SUSTAINED ACTIVE PARTICIPATION OF 3.5% DID NOT FAIL. CHENOWETH LATER STRESSED THAT THIS IS A TENDENCY FROM A SPECIFIC CLASS OF CAMPAIGNS, NOT A LAW.
- Applied to today's world population, that is about 280M people. But an online global treaty vote is not sustained national participation. Treat 280 million as a historical benchmark, not a promise or finish line.
- The public coordination target remains 4B.
- [Read Chenoweth's cautionary update](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/2024-05/Erica%20Chenoweth_2020-005.pdf)
#### IF ONE MILLION PEOPLE START
- ~247 DAYS
- If each new voter brings 1.1 more voters in the next 7-day round
- ~119 DAYS
- If each new voter brings 1.3 more voters in the next 7-day round
- ~33 DAYS
- If each new voter brings 1.6 more voters in the next 3-day round
- ~24 DAYS
- If each new voter brings 2 more voters in the next 3-day round
- These are geometric illustrations, not forecasts. Real propagation loses people to overlap, verification, language, access, and ordinary human failure to click links.
