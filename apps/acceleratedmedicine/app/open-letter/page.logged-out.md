## Open Letter
- Universal Care-Integrated Clinical Trials
- Every patient should have the right and practical ability to:
- Visit their own doctor.
- Compare treatment rankings and Outcome Labels showing benefits and harms.
- Join trials of promising treatments through ordinary care.
- Have every result improve the evidence for the next patient.
- We call on policymakers, health systems, and research funders to:
- Enable participation through ordinary care: put the policies and trial infrastructure in place so patients can join through their own doctor.
- Fund clinics' research work: pay for the extra staff time, monitoring, and outcome reporting needed to make participation practical.
- Make every result count: require timely public reporting of positive, negative, and inconclusive results, with patient privacy protected.
