## ABOUT WISHOCRACY
- What should public money pay for? Wishocracy lets you set your priorities and compare them with government spending.
### TWO PRIORITIES AT A TIME
- Choose the programs you want to fund. Then divide a hypothetical $100 between two programs at a time. Your choices become a percentage allocation you can see and change on the results page.
### HOW TO READ THE RESULTS
- Each answer splits $100 between two programs. We pool everyone's latest answer for each pair and find the one set of shares that fits them all best, using the method in the Wishocracy paper. Answering "fund neither", or skipping a program when you pick what to fund, counts as giving it nothing. The results page ranks the programs by these shares and compares that order with the government's order by spending. The likely range shows how far a rank could move if a different group of people had answered. Your own result uses only your answers and covers only the programs you compared.
- The government's order comes from annual federal spending on the same programs, not the entire federal budget.
- [Explore the results](/results)
### THE RESEARCH
- The Wishocracy paper explores how pairwise preferences could guide public budgets and measure how closely governments follow citizens' priorities. It describes the broader research framework, including the calculation explained above.
