## THE PLAN
- The steps below are the machinery. You do not need to build the machinery. You need to turn it on. Here is the switch.
- This page is the short version. The manual has the details.
### THE PLAN IN 6 STEPS
- THE SHORT VERSION OF HOW THIS IS SUPPOSED TO WORK
#### START WITH THE PEOPLE WHO ALREADY KNOW WHAT WAITING 443.3 YEARS COSTS
- Patient organizations share the vote with their members — the specific humans who have already done the math on what it costs to wait 443.3 years.
#### HELP MEMBERS SPREAD THE WORD
- You are not creating support for not dying of horrible diseases. Nearly everyone already supports that. You are proving it.
#### REACH THE NUMBER WHERE "I'LL LOOK INTO IT" STOPS WORKING
- The vote is the moment every human who assumed they were the weird one finds out they are everyone. The target is 4B.
#### TRADE 1% OF MILITARY SPENDING FOR TRIALS
- Every country gains from every other country complying. That is not how arms treaties usually work. The math happened to work out that way.
#### RUN 12X MORE TRIALS
- Instead of testing on 200 humans in clinical terrariums, test on real patients in real hospitals. Where the diseases are.
#### COLLECT CURES THAT WERE OTHERWISE 443.3 YEARS AWAY
- Those are individual humans who currently have plans for next Tuesday.
