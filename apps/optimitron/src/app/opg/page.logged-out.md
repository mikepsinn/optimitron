### [Right to Trial & FDA Upgrade Act](/opg/right-to-trial-and-fda-upgrade-act)

- Draft legislation; most patients ineligible for conventional trials; 6,650 diseases with zero FDA-approved treatments
- Enact patient trial-access rights plus a real-time FDA outcome-label platform (dFDA)
